01The bottleneck

The world is running out of accumulated experience.

Every consequential decision draws on a stock of experience — situations seen, causes tested, mistakes survived. That stock accumulates one career at a time, while the decisions drawing on it multiply and mutate faster every year.

And the record keeps the wrong thing. Business history logs outcomes — the deal closed, the plant opened, the quarter missed. It almost never logs the reasoning: the considerations weighed, the assumptions carried, the alternatives declined. The one thing a decision-maker most needs from the past is precisely what the past does not record.

Frontier models inherit the same gap. They can search, reason, use tools and run agents — and still miss consequential parts of a decision space, because that experience was never written anywhere they could learn it.

02The method

Manufacture the experience.
Measure the coverage.
Ground it in the company.

We do not generate synthetic text. We generate synthetic experience — worlds, mechanisms, decisions, failures and consequences that a specialist can learn from. The machine discovers the decision space as it generates, and stops only when measured coverage saturates.

I

Map the space

Business decisions don't come with a map. The machine partitions the decision space as it explores it — including the regions where nothing has happened yet.

II

Generate experience

From minimal seeds, it expands deep decision sequences — context, trade-offs, consequences — across the whole mapped space, not just around the starting sample.

III

Measure coverage

Coverage of the mapped space is measured, continuously. Generation does not halt on a schedule or a sample size; it halts when the measurement saturates.

IV

Ground the company

Domain training teaches a specialist the space of worlds that could exist. A company's own evidence then progressively locates it in the one that does — and every outcome makes it more specific.

Mbase + Ddomain → Mdomain   ·   Mdomain + Dcompany → Mdomain@company   ·   trained across possible worlds, grounded in the real one

03Measured so far

Early, small-scale — and pointing one way.

These are pilot-scale measurements, each reported with its limits. What full scale looks like is the work ahead — not a claim we make today.

every concept vs ≤22%
Twenty generated sequences from a small open model reached every tracked concept in four domains; a single unbounded multi-agent frontier run with tools reached at most 22% of the same space.
measured · pilot scale · aug 2026
92 vs 42
On one real investment decision, ~1,000 generated sequences in context produced 92 exclusive high-impact considerations; an unrestricted agentic frontier run alone produced 42 — including the study's single top insight, reached only with our data.
measured · one decision · blind-scored
94% of 27B
A 2B model taught one planning step from our generated plans reached 94% of its 27B teacher on fine-grained work, 18 of 18 cases — and sampled eight times, its best answer equals the teacher outright.
measured · micro-scale transfer · blind-judged

Run blind against a frontier-built causal model of a capital decision, discovery surfaced five missing mechanisms that reversed the modeled economics of the decision.

05Where this goes

Models built to compound.
