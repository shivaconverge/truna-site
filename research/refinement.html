Compounding refinement

An expert works in both directions: forward from what is known, backward from what is needed. R³SI makes those two directions exchange evidence — and reuses every verified improvement as the next starting point. The question: does the work compound?

measured one task model-reviewed

Setup

The test bed is a problem where results can be checked: a revenue guarantee in mechanism design — how close simple selling strategies come to the best possible mechanism for one buyer of many items, expressed as a coefficient where lower is stronger. Forward reasoning derives consequences of the current proof; backward reasoning proposes what would have to be true for a stronger one. Reviewed lemmas close subgoals between them; a prover then writes a complete proof, a separate model reviews it, and only a verified improvement becomes the next seed. Model weights never change.

Result

Four accepted improvements took a seeded guarantee from 3.740 to 3.155 — about 10% stronger than a recently published agentic result (3.520) and about 70% of the way to the best published mathematical guarantee of 3. A proof-only control from the same seed reached 3.354.

Why it matters

Generating ideas is easy; accumulating them is not. Here partial results, failed routes and reviewed lemmas became a research state that the next cycle started from — and the guarantee improved four times in a row. Mathematics is the laboratory because a proof can be checked; the mechanism — reason forward from the world, backward from the decision, keep only what survives review — is the one our specialists are being built around.

This establishes

  • An agentic loop accumulated four verified improvements on one proof, with no weight updates.
  • The final guarantee beats a recently published agentic result and far exceeds direct prompting (4.829).
  • Every step is auditable: complete proof, reviewed dependencies, exact rational certificates.

Not yet shown

  • New mathematics — the published factor-3 result is stronger.
  • That the system itself improves; the gains are in the work, not the model.
  • A matched-compute advantage over the control, or results across many tasks.
  • Formal verification — reviews are by a separate model.
