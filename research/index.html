Across the studies, established

  • Space-first generation covers concept regions that unbounded frontier runs do not enter, at pilot scale.
  • Generated experience in context materially changes what a frontier model considers on a real decision.
  • Blind discovery can surface consequential structure missing from a frontier-built model of a decision.
  • A first micro-scale transfer of a planning skill into a small model's weights, blind-judged against its teacher.

Not yet established

  • That these effects hold at full training scale — the central experiment ahead.
  • That they generalise across many unrelated decisions; the current case-study evidence is deliberately small.
  • That discovered magnitudes are correct in the real world — they are estimates that a mechanism matters, not measurements of its size.
  • That a specialist beats strong baselines on frozen historical decisions under blind, independent judging — the evaluation we are building toward.
